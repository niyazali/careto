# Retrieval and source verification records

The manuscript reports the author-confirmed initial search date of 21 July 2026. Raw API execution timestamps are retained without alteration in retrieval_receipts/search_log.jsonl; the author correction and its basis are recorded separately in search_date_corrections.json. Targeted searches on 6–10 October 2026 extended the corpus.

Initial acquisition counts are not a unified full-text screening flow. The historical full-text queues and manual adjudication stages cover different acquisition sets; they are not retrospectively combined into a PRISMA flow. The current main analysis retains 450 observations from 64 original-study groups.

Screening, extraction and reconciliation were AI assisted. Scripted consolidation and primary-source checking support the retained numerical values, analytes, treatments, units and endpoint definitions. The records do not establish two independent human screeners or extractors. Primary-source evidence includes accessible article text, tables and figures, with identifiable graph estimates explicitly flagged. Unresolved source or amount-basis candidates were not forced into the main cohort.

Raw source labels and response values are unchanged. ANALYTE_CANONICALIZATION.json describes the conservative model-label mapping, and analyte_model_labels.csv pairs each source label with its model label. Unspecified isomers, position-specific cis species, free/total/ester pools and differently defined mixtures remain separate. The mapping corrects model encoding; it does not recalculate analytical outcomes.
