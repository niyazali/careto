# Source expansion

The main dataset adds 36 terminal isolated-micellar observations from one quinoa study, DOI 10.1021/acs.jafc.6c01729. Treatment-level table means, endpoint equation, separation methods and study grouping were verified before fitting. The original 414 main observations were preserved exactly. One reported mean exceeds 100% and is retained under the magnitude-independent rule. Undetected compounds and pooled genotype summaries are excluded.

The quantitative subset expands from 40 observations in seven studies to 81 observations in 10 studies already represented in the main dataset. This adds covariates to existing response records. All feature comparisons and outcome values are retained. Candidate grids, seed, primary response definition and full-dataset model descriptors are unchanged.

Additional sparse-category sources were screened but not admitted when the endpoint, treatment match or concentration basis was unresolved. The main paper and supplement of 10.1039/D2FO00611A were reviewed. Fraction isolation and the nominal initial-pasta denominator were confirmed, but the common amount basis remains ambiguous because concentrations are reported on a dry-weight basis after separate fractions were freeze-dried. The 117 reported means and full-text decision are preserved separately in screened_sources; they were not admitted to any fitted cohort. These entries did not enter any fitted cohort. The supplied article 10.1016/j.crfs.2023.100500 is an existing food-composition comparator and supplies no digestion outcomes. No new experimental validation was performed.
